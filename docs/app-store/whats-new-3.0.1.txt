What’s New in Version 3.0.1

• Add end-to-end encrypted circle syncing so shared dining logs stay current across devices.
• Sign in with Apple and use single-use invitations to securely join a dining circle.
• See clear connection status, first-sync progress, connected-member app versions, and recent activity.
• Rename a circle and manage its members directly from Settings or the More tab.
• Keep simultaneous edits, deletions, large first syncs, and encrypted photo transfers reliable.
• Preserve dining history and photos from previously accepted iCloud circles during the upgrade.
